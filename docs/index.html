How to read this
Gamma regime
Positive gamma means options dealers hedge against the move, which tends to calm price and keep it in a range. Negative gamma means they hedge with the move, which tends to make moves extend.
Call wall
The strike with the most call gamma. It often acts as a ceiling or magnet on the way up.
Put wall
The strike with the most put gamma. It often acts as a floor or magnet on the way down.
Zero-gamma flip
The price where dealer positioning switches from positive to negative gamma. Above it moves tend to slow down, below it they tend to speed up.

What you need to know today

    Options data is about 15 minutes delayed, open interest updates once a day, and positions opened today (including same-day 0DTE trades) aren't included. GEX is context, not a trade signal.
